GOC link grabber

Turns your Games Over Coffee videos into lines like Episode title | https://youtu.be/…, ready to paste into the curator's Staff → Friday features.

The quick way: a bookmark

  1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar: Grab GOC videos
  2. Open your YouTube channel's Videos tab (or any playlist) and scroll down until every video you want has loaded.
  3. Click the bookmark. It copies every video on the page, newest first, and tells you how many it found.
  4. Paste into Friday features, or below to check it.

It only reads the page you're on and copies to your clipboard. Shorts are skipped. If your browser won't let it copy, it shows the list in a box to copy by hand.

Or: paste links

Paste YouTube links, or any text with links in it (one per line is fine, so is a jumble). It looks up each title for you.
