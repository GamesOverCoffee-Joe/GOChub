Walk with Arrow keys or WASD, or click to walk if it's on in Settings. Look, talk and tidy with Z or Space. X goes back, or takes a photo of whatever's in front of you. Enter or Esc opens the menu (your stuff, controls, save). A game controller works too. Dust the frames, water the plants, find the curator's mug, and turn out the lights when you leave.

SETTINGS

Tap to walkTap where you want to go. Tap a piece, a person, a prop or a door to walk over and use it. The D-pad and keys always work too.
On-screen controlsAutomatic shows them on touch screens. Hide them if you've connected a keyboard or controller to a tablet.
Sharp pixelsOnly zoom the game by whole numbers (2x, 3x...) so every pixel stays crisp. On a phone held upright that can make it quite small.
Swap A and BFor Nintendo-style controllers, where A and B are the other way around. Swaps the keyboard's Z and X too.
Gameplay videoMuted video in the museum: this hour's episode on the screening nook's screen, and gameplay beside the placards that have it. Turn it off to save data and battery.
Vibrate on button pressesA tiny buzz when you press the D-pad, A or B.
